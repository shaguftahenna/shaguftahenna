GAN Module — UDL template in practice

The GANs module is the representative example of the UDL module template applied uniformly across all 18 modules. It demonstrates multi-modal content delivery, live simulation integration, and structured per-student reflection with localStorage persistence.

Students enter their name and student ID, engage with the GANLab simulation or lecture video, then complete two guided reflection prompts. Submissions are stored under the key format GANs_studentID_reflection1 in localStorage, ensuring persistence across sessions without server-side data collection.
