Research Ireland — ARC Hub for ICT Principal Investigator · ~€500,000 · 3 projects

ARC Hub for ICT — Applied Research & Commercialisation Programme
~€500,000
Principal Investigator Research Ireland (formerly SFI) 3 concurrent projects · each >€150,000 Applied AI · 6G Networks · IoHT Security

Currently leading three significant research projects as PI, each valued at over €150,000, under the prestigious ARC Hub Programme — combined total approximately €500,000. The ARC (Applied Research and Commercialisation) Hub, funded by Research Ireland, catalyses regional innovation by bridging the gap between publicly funded research and real-world commercial impact, working with 11 research institutions across Ireland.

Each project is strategically aligned with the National Smart Specialisation Strategy for Innovation 2022–2027 (S3), focusing on applied AI, predictive analytics, and intelligent systems to address regional industry challenges. These initiatives position the work at the intersection of academia, industry, and innovation — supporting a new national model for impactful, regionally grounded research commercialisation.

Projects under this programme

  • TP1 — RANControl: Reinforced structural learning-based xApps for Open RAN closed-loop control on programmable experimental platforms.
  • TP2 — 6GOrch: Low variance deep learning framework for joint resource orchestration in 6G extreme edge computing.
  • TP3 — FedHealthSecure: Few-shot federated learning with variance reduction for ransomware attack detection in Internet of Health Things (IoHTs).

Interreg Europe — WeSTEMEU Co-Investigator · €220,000 · 6 EU regions

WeSTEMEU — Women in STEM Employment in the EU
€220,000
Co-Investigator Interreg Europe · EU Co-funded 6 EU regions Gender Equity · STEM Policy Ireland partner: Donegal County Council

Actively involved in the WeSTEMEU project, funded by Interreg Europe, addressing a critical gap in gender equity within STEM fields across Europe. The project focuses on enhancing and influencing policy instruments to facilitate women's access to employment in STEM sectors, contributing to more inclusive and effective labour markets.

Operating across six EU regions — Catalonia (Spain), Lithuania, Bucharest (Romania), Western Greece, Podkarpackie (Poland), and Donegal (Ireland) — promoting interregional knowledge exchange and best practice transfer. Female representation in science and technology ranges from 23% to 52% across EU member states; the project directly targets these disparities.

In the Irish strand, in collaboration with Donegal County Council, the work investigates existing barriers and policies, engaging closely with schools, SMEs, and policymakers to refine policy instruments for a more equitable STEM landscape. The long-term goal is to reduce the gender gap, improve employment quality for women, and advance innovation and sustainable development by making the STEM workforce more representative.

WeSTEMEU will ultimately contribute to ameliorating gender imbalance in STEM — making the sector more responsive to social, economic, and environmental needs — while generating economic returns through jobs growth, innovation, and closing the pay gap as women enter higher-paid STEM roles.

TU RISE — Government of Ireland / EU ERDF Principal Investigator · €350,000

TU RISE — AI & Digital Twins for Ocean, Coastal & Agricultural Sustainability
€350,000
Principal Investigator Government of Ireland EU ERDF — Southern, Eastern & Midland Regional Programme 2021–2027 PRTP · COAST · MATX platforms

Secured research funding through the TU RISE initiative, co-funded by the Government of Ireland and the European Union via the ERDF Southern, Eastern & Midland Regional Programme 2021–2027, valued at approximately €350,000, awarded through the PRTP, COAST, and MATX platforms.

Research focuses on the integration of Artificial Intelligence with Digital Twin technologies across key sustainability domains — ocean sustainability, coastal resilience, and smart agriculture and farming — developing intelligent, data-driven systems to model, simulate, and optimise environmental and agricultural processes, contributing to long-term regional and global sustainability goals.

MOCHAS — PRTP, Atlantic Technological University Principal Investigator · €200,000

MOCHAS — Modelling and Computation for Health and Society
€200,000
Principal Investigator Postgraduate Research Training Programme (PRTP) Atlantic Technological University Graph Deep Learning · Healthcare · Environmental Monitoring

Secured €200,000 under the Postgraduate Research Training Programme (PRTP) in Modelling and Computation for Health and Society (MOCHAS) at ATU. This interdisciplinary, cohort-based programme produces high-calibre researchers equipped with both technical and transferable skills to address real-world societal challenges through advanced modelling and computational tools.

Research contributes to developing scalable, interpretable AI models for applications including pollution tracking, ecological resilience, and predictive diagnostics. President's Seed Fund — Letterkenny Institute of Technology Principal Investigator · €200,000

President's Seed Fund — Graph Deep Learning for Cybersecurity
€200,000
Principal Investigator Letterkenny Institute of Technology (LYIT) Cybersecurity · Federated Learning · APT Detection

Secured €200,000 through the President's Seed Fund at Letterkenny Institute of Technology to support research in AI-driven cybersecurity. Research spans two distinct streams:

  • Federated learning and differential privacy using graph neural networks for robust, privacy-preserving malware detection across distributed environments without centralised data sharing.
  • Graph-based deep reinforcement learning for detecting and mitigating Advanced Persistent Threats (APTs), enabling adaptive and proactive cyber defence strategies.

This initiative aligns with national cybersecurity strategies and contributes to the development of next-generation AI solutions for critical infrastructure protection.
