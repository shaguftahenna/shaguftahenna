Port of Galway — Digital Infrastructure & Innovation Consultant
Jan 2025 – Present
Innovation Consultant Port of Galway Digital Infrastructure 6G · Fibre Connectivity

Engaged as Digital Infrastructure and Innovation Consultant for the Port of Galway, providing strategic expertise on the adoption of advanced connectivity technologies to drive digital transformation and innovation at the port.

Key activities & contributions

  • Assessing strategic benefits and potential applications of low-latency, high-throughput fibre connectivity for port operations, research, and innovation
  • Researching current and projected fibre infrastructure across Galway, Ireland, and internationally by engaging with network providers and service operators
  • Analysing global case studies of ports leveraging fibre connectivity to drive digital transformation, identifying applicable models for Galway
  • Preparing detailed reports and presentations on fibre connectivity opportunities, incorporating findings from industry stakeholder interviews
  • Leading market research activities including surveys and interviews to identify digital infrastructure needs and future priorities
  • Evaluating alignment between stakeholder demand and existing or planned connectivity infrastructure to support strategic planning
  • Exploring emerging applications such as private 6G networks and testbeds enabled by advanced connectivity at the port
  • Developing strategic insights and recommendations to position Galway as a digitally enabled, innovation-driven port hub

ARC Hub for ICT — Principal Investigator Research Ireland · AI Commercialisation

Research Ireland ARC Hub for ICT
Principal Investigator
Principal Investigator Research Ireland AI Commercialisation Technology Validation Industry Adoption

As Principal Investigator within the Research Ireland ARC Hub for ICT, Dr. Henna leads and contributes to multiple projects focused on AI commercialisation, technology validation, and industry adoption — particularly in telecommunications, digital infrastructure, and intelligent systems.

Commercialisation focus areas

  • Graph-based deep learning solutions for network function virtualisation in industrial environments
  • Explainable AI frameworks for telecommunications and digital infrastructure
  • Federated learning solutions for secure IoT and healthcare data processing
  • Digital twin technologies for intelligent infrastructure monitoring
  • AI-native network architecture aligned with 5G/6G global standards (ITU-T FG-ML5G)

CONNECT Centre — Research Fellow Graph-based Deep Learning · 2018–2019

CONNECT Centre Research Fellow — Graph-based Deep Learning / ML
Sep 2018 – Sep 2019
Research Fellow CONNECT Centre Ireland Graph Neural Networks Pre-Commercialisation NFV · Autoscaling

Led research on Graph Neural Networks (GNNs) for autoscaling in service function chains within virtualised networks, focusing on scalability, efficiency, and real-world applicability. Contributed directly to the pre-commercialisation phase of graph-based deep learning for network systems.

Key contributions

  • Research & Development: Led research on GNNs for autoscaling in service function chains within virtualised networks
  • Algorithm Design: Developed and optimised algorithms using GNNs to manage resource allocation and enhance network service performance
  • Data Analysis: Collected, analysed, and modelled network data to produce data-driven, real-world applicable models
  • Industry Collaboration: Worked closely with industry partners to align research with practical needs and technological challenges
  • Prototype Development: Designed and implemented prototypes demonstrating viability of graph-based deep learning for autoscaling in network systems
  • Pre-Commercialisation & Trials: Contributed to sandbox trials to test model feasibility and gather feedback for further improvements
  • Business Model Support: Assisted in developing a business model for spinout opportunities, evaluating commercialisation potential and strategic direction
