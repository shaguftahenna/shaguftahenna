WeSTEMEU — European STEM Inclusion Initiative 2022–2026 · 6 EU Regions · Co-Investigator

WeSTEMEU: Advancing Women in STEM Across Europe
Co-Investigator Interreg Europe 2022–2026 Policy Influence 6 EU Regions

WeSTEMEU addresses a critical structural gap in gender equity within STEM across Europe. Female representation in science and technology varies from just 23% to 52% across EU member states — the project facilitates interregional knowledge exchange and best-practice transfer to reduce this disparity.

Working in collaboration with Donegal County Council, Dr. Henna investigates existing barriers and policies in Ireland, refining policy instruments to create a more inclusive STEM environment — engaging directly with schools, SMEs, public authorities, and innovation agencies.

🇷🇴 Bucharest, Romania

Policy & impact objectives

  • Influence regional policy instruments to facilitate women's access to STEM employment across 6 EU regions
  • Transfer best practices from high-performing regions (up to 52% women in STEM) to lower-performing ones (as low as 23%)
  • Contribute to closing the gender pay gap by enabling women to access higher-paid STEM careers
  • Drive sustainable development and innovation through a more gender-representative STEM workforce
  • Support EU priorities for inclusive growth, regional innovation, and gender-responsive economic development
