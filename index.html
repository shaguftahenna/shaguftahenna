Research & Practice

Principal Investigator at the Research Ireland ARC Hub for ICT and WiSAR Lab, ATU. Research spans explainable AI, generative AI, digital twins, self-supervised learning, and agentic AI, applied to healthcare analytics, cybersecurity, smart agriculture, and intelligent infrastructure.

Inclusive Pedagogy

Universal Design for Learning practitioner, accessibility and inclusion embedded from the first line of design, not retrofitted. Holder of the national UDL Digital Badge and three postgraduate qualifications in education and inclusive pedagogy, with practice grounded in CAST UDL 3.0.

Scholarship & Impact

Sustained scholarly engagement across AI research, commercialisation, and pedagogy. Competitive research funding secured as PI/Co-PI through national and European programmes. Co-Investigator on Interreg Europe initiatives supporting policy development for women in STEM and inclusive innovation across European regions.
